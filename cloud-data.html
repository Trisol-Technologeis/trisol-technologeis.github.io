Summary

This page declares how apps published by OBanaf Apps Studio access, use, store and share Google user data and cloud-stored data. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

OBanaf Apps Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Google sign-in, Drive backup and Calendar sync are optional. Every app works without them.
  • Your backups are stored in your own Google Drive — never on our servers.
  • We never sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or let humans read it.

Where cloud data is stored

  • Drive backups are stored in your Google Drive account, encrypted by Google, and count toward your own Drive storage.
  • Calendar events are stored in your Google Calendar.
  • Access tokens are kept on your device in Android’s secure storage and are never sent to our servers.
  • All transfers between the app and Google use HTTPS/TLS encryption.

Sharing & Limited Use

Google user data obtained through our apps:

  • is used only to provide or improve the user-facing backup, restore and sync features described above;
  • is not transferred to third parties, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your notice;
  • is not used for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalised or interest-based ads;
  • is not sold, and is not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending;
  • is not read by humans, unless you give explicit permission for specific data (for example, a support request), it is needed for security or abuse investigation, or required by law;
  • is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Revoking access

  1. In the app, open Settings → Backup & Sync and tap Sign out / Disconnect.
  2. Or visit myaccount.google.com/connections, select the app, and choose Delete all connections.

Revoking access stops all future access immediately. Existing backups remain in your Drive until you delete them.

Request data deletion

You can delete your data at any time, with or without contacting us:

Delete it yourself

  • Drive backups — in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find the app and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • On-device data — in Android Settings → Apps → [app] → Storage, tap Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising ID — in Android Settings → Google → Ads, tap Delete advertising ID.

Ask us to delete it

Email info@pandezlabsltd.site with the subject “Data deletion request”, the app name, and (if you used Google sign-in) the Google account email. We will delete any data associated with you — including analytics records linked to your installation and any support correspondence — within 30 days and confirm by email.

We do not keep any data after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep limited records (for example, purchase records handled by Google Play).
