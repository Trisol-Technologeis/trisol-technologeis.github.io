Who we are & scope

OBanaf Apps Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) publishes the Android applications listed below on Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains what information our apps collect, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to every app published under our Google Play developer account, including:

  • Ethiopian Calendar (ቀን መቁጠሪያ)
  • Nepali Patro – Nepali Calendar
  • Persian Calendar تقویم فارسی
  • AI Math Solver With Solution
  • Smart Printer App: Mobile Print
  • HD Camera Beauty And Filter
  • Pose Camera Photo Guide
  • Dress AI : AI Try On
  • AI Gift Finder

By installing or using our apps you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

Information we collect

Information collected automatically

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, country/region and screen size.
  • App usage & diagnostics — screens viewed, features used, session length, crash logs and performance data.
  • Device identifiers — the Android Advertising ID and Firebase installation IDs, used for analytics and advertising.

Information you choose to provide

  • Photos and camera images — when you take or select a photo to scan, edit, solve, apply a filter or try on an outfit.
  • Text you enter — math questions, gift-finder answers, calendar events, reminders and notes.
  • Files and documents — documents, PDFs and images you choose to print or scan.
  • Contact details — your email address and message, only if you contact us for support.

We do not ask for your name, postal address or phone number, and we do not create user accounts on our own servers.

How we use information

  • To provide app features you request, such as converting dates, solving a problem, generating a try-on image or printing a document.
  • To keep apps stable and secure: diagnosing crashes, fixing bugs and preventing abuse.
  • To understand which features are used so we can improve them.
  • To show advertising that keeps our apps free, and to measure how ads perform.
  • To process in-app purchases and subscriptions through Google Play.
  • To reply to you when you contact support.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose that is unrelated to our apps.

AI features & your photos

Some apps offer AI features — AI Math Solver, Dress AI, AI Gift Finder and AI effects in our camera apps. When you use one of these features:

  • The photo or text you submit is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection to an AI processing service solely to produce your result.
  • Submitted content is processed temporarily and is not used to build a profile of you, sold, or shared for advertising.
  • We do not keep your uploaded photos after the result has been generated, except where the app explicitly lets you save results to your own device.

Photos, videos and files that you do not submit to an AI feature stay on your device.

Advertising & consent

Free versions of our apps show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s Advertising ID and similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance.

  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google’s consent message before personalised ads are shown.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Purchasing an ad-free option, where available, removes ads from that app.

Google user data

Some apps let you optionally sign in with Google to back up your data to Google Drive or sync events with Google Calendar. This data is accessed only after you grant permission, is used only to provide that feature, and is stored in your own Google account — not on our servers.

Full details of the scopes we request, how the data is stored and how to revoke access are in our Cloud Storage & Google User Data Declaration.

Data security

  • All data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • We follow data-minimisation: we collect only what a feature needs and process it on-device whenever possible.
  • Access to analytics and crash dashboards is restricted to our team.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards.

Retention & deletion

  • On-device data (events, reminders, settings, saved results) stays on your device and is removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • AI submissions are deleted after your result is generated.
  • Analytics & crash data are kept for up to 14 months by Firebase, then deleted automatically.
  • Cloud backups live in your Google Drive and can be deleted by you at any time.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

To ask us to delete any data associated with you, follow the steps in Request data deletion.

Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. We do not sell or “share” personal information as defined by the CCPA.

To exercise any right, email us — we respond within 30 days. You can also manage permissions and ad settings on your device at any time.

Children’s privacy

Our apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our apps change. The “Last updated” date at the top will show when it was last revised, and significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
