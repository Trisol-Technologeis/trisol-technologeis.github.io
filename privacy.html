Privacy Policy

This policy explains how apps published by OBanaf Apps Studio on Google Play (“the Apps”) handle information. By using the Apps you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Content you provide — such as photos you choose to scan, edit or try on, or questions you type. This is processed only to deliver the feature you requested.
  • Device & usage data — anonymous diagnostics, crash reports and app interactions collected through services such as Google Firebase.
  • Advertising identifiers — some Apps show ads via Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID to serve and measure ads.

Permissions

Camera, storage, notification or nearby-device permissions are requested only when a feature needs them (for example scanning a document, saving a photo, calendar reminders or connecting a printer). You can revoke them anytime in Android settings.

How we use information

To provide and improve app features, fix crashes, show relevant ads and process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

The Apps may use Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics, Google AdMob and Google Play Billing. Each is governed by its own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Data retention & deletion

Photos and inputs are not stored on our servers longer than needed to process a request. To request deletion of any data associated with you, email us.
